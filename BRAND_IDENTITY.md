## Target Markets

### Primary: English-Speaking Tourists
- Travelers visiting Cusco, Machu Picchu, Sacred Valley
- Looking for authentic, local coffee experiences
- Will pay premium pricing (USD)
- Discovery channels: Google Search, TripAdvisor, Hostelworld, Instagram, walking past

### Secondary: Local Peruvian Market
- Cusco residents, Lima coffee enthusiasts
- Spanish-speaking
- Pricing in Peruvian Soles (PEN)
- Discovery: Instagram, word of mouth, local events

### Tertiary: B2B / Wholesale
- International roasters seeking direct-trade Peruvian green beans
- Cafés in Peru needing wholesale supply
- Language: English/Spanish

### Pricing
- **Dual currency:** USD (website/tourists) + PEN (local market)
- Products display both currencies

## Product Lines

### 1. Roasted Specialty Coffee
- Small-batch roasted in Cusco
- Single-origin lots from La Convención and Cajamarca
- SCA scored, Q-grader profiled
- Sold online (purplebean.coffee) and via WhatsApp delivery

### 2. Green Beans (Wholesale)
- Direct-trade green coffee for roasters
- Full traceability to farm and lot level
- Available in bulk quantities

### 3. Coffee Subscriptions
- Recurring deliveries of seasonal specialty coffee
- Target: both local and tourist/expat markets
